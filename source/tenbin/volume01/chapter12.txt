Episode 12: Himeko’s Best Friend
Himeko returned home just before 7 p.m., as the sun was setting.

“I’m back!”

“Welcome home. Where’s Haruki?” Hayato asked.

“She’s too tired to eat here, said she’ll manage.”

“Haruki, out of energy? What were you two doing?”

“Just shopping?”

Hayato eyed Himeko suspiciously, but her glowing, satisfied expression betrayed her. Sniffing the air, she checked dinner.

“Curry?”

“Yup, enough for tomorrow night too.”

“Tell me when it’s ready.”

“Sure.”

In her room, Himeko tossed her bag aside and flopped onto the bed, hugging her pillow and rolling around. Grinning, she replayed the day’s events, basking in the afterglow.

Her first outing in the city was a shock. Unlike rural Tsukino-se, the trains were numerous, the station complex, and the streets filled with diverse cars—not just pickup trucks. Towering buildings stretched skyward, the epitome of the urban dream she’d envisioned. Her excitement had soared since morning.

“Wow, where do we even start…?”

“Hehe, follow Central Street’s shops, hit the department store at the end, then loop the perimeter—efficient and thorough!” Haruki chirped.

“Haru-chan, you were reluctant yesterday, but you’re super into this now?”

“When I commit, I go all out! Plus, it’ll surprise Hayato, right?”

“True.”

Haruki’s enthusiasm outshone Himeko’s. Her mischievous smile, paired with a breezy tunic and shorts—cute yet refined—made Himeko’s heart skip. Himeko’s styling choice, with the tunic being her own, amplified the effect.

(All out for Onii, huh…)

“Imagine Haruki in a super girly outfit, totally flustering Onii—wouldn’t that be hilarious?”

Himeko’s whispered scheme at Haruki’s house had sparked this. Her goal was to tease Hayato, so Haruki’s reaction was expected. Still, a twinge of jealousy stirred in Himeko, which she brushed off by slapping her cheeks.

“Nice spirit, Hime-chan!”

“Let’s hit every shop!”

“Yeah!”

The hyped duo scoured stores, occasionally sidetracked by Haruki’s dives into game shops, a yellow submarine store, or gacha machines—charmingly predictable.

At a famous fruit parlor, they devoured jumbo fruit parfaits as big as their faces, chatting away. Their plan to browse systematically faltered as they backtracked to mix and match items, stretching the day far beyond schedule. Haruki, buzzing with energy, crashed on the train home, head on Himeko’s shoulder, utterly defenseless. Himeko couldn’t help but smile.

(Today was so fun… Oh?)

Lost in memories, a phone notification snapped her back. It was Saki, her Tsukino-se best friend.

“Whatcha do today? I was so bored, binged train scenery videos from around the world~”

“We’re third-years, Saki! Study for exams!”

“Weird, right? Sat to study, ended up like this~”

“Well, I was shopping all day.”

“Cool~ What’d you buy?”

“More like chose stuff… Remember Haru-chan, who hung out with Onii a lot?”

“Yup, the one your brother said powered up from monkey to gorilla~?”

“Exactly. We were picking clothes for her… Hang on.”

Himeko sifted through photos, selecting favorites to send: casual, feminine, and sweet girly outfits, all flattering both her and Haruki, highlighting their charm. Pleased with her shots, she also wanted to stun Saki with Haruki’s transformed beauty—a playful surprise, just a conversation starter.

“!?”

A call from Saki, almost simultaneous with the photos, caught her off guard.

“Uh, Saki-cha—”

“Who’s this girl~?”

“W-What, Saki-chan!? It’s Haru-chan!”

“Really~? Quite the beauty~.”

“Y-Yeah, a beauty, right?”

Saki’s voice, usually timid and laid-back, was chillingly intense. Instinctively, Himeko sat upright on her bed, rigid.

“Did you lie to me, Hime-chan~? I heard gorilla~.”

“N-No, that’s true too! She’s still the same inside, like back then—careless, rough, totally gorilla around Onii!”

“Like old times~!?”

“Saki-chan!?”

Himeko was bewildered. She’d never heard gentle Saki so rattled, and the reason eluded her. Tearful snippets—“High school plans~,” “Summer break there~,” “Teen boys are beasts~”—left her panicking.

“Oi, Himeko, it’s ready!” Hayato called.

A lifeline.

“Sorry, Saki-chan, dinner’s calling!”

“Hime-chan~!?”

“Onii, coming!”

Apologizing inwardly, Himeko fled, still puzzled by Saki’s reaction.
