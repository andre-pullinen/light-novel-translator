Prologue
It was another hot day.

In the tranquil mountain village of Tsukinose, time seemed detached from the world. The blazing midsummer sun shone brightly, with white cumulonimbus clouds peeking from the mountains.

At the village outskirts, near a small roadside shrine, three children, shirts clinging to their sweaty skin, stared intently at something.

“I’ll chase it from the right side of the shrine, Haruki, you hide ahead and ambush it.”

“Okay, make sure you drive it toward me!”

“Of course! Don’t mess up, alright?”

“You too, Hayato!”

They exchanged small grins and focused ahead.

In front of the shrine was a lamb, about the size of a medium dog, basking leisurely in the sun. It had escaped from Grandpa Gen’s house that morning. The kids were the “Little Lamb Search Team.”

“Haru-chan, big brother, what about me…?”

Unlike the eager Hayato and Haruki, Himeko fidgeted nervously.

“Hmm, Hime-chan, how about you block the way here?”

“Yeah, if Himeko stays there, the lamb will head toward Haruki.”

“O-Okay!”

Thus, the lamb-capture strategy began.

“Here we go, Haruki!”

Startled by Hayato’s sudden appearance and voice, the lamb froze for a moment before darting, as planned, along the shrine toward Haruki’s hiding spot. The two boys smirked in triumph.

“Gotcha!”

But the lamb, spooked by Haruki’s sudden leap, sharply changed direction.

In that instant, everyone gasped.

“Himeko!?” “Hime-chan!?”

“Eek!?”

“Baa!?”

Himeko stood there, frozen in shock as the lamb charged toward her, its momentum unstoppable. Unable to move, she squeezed her eyes shut.

“~~~!”

“Hime-chan! …Ow!”

“Haruki!?”

A loud thud echoed. Himeko cautiously opened her eyes to see Haruki, knocked back and sitting on the ground, and the lamb, which had tumbled over.

Hayato rushed over.

“I-I’m fine! Hime-chan too! So, grab the lamb!”

“Got it!”

Hayato hesitated briefly but, meeting Haruki’s gaze, nodded and lunged, capturing the lamb.

“Himeko, go tell Grandpa Gen!”

“Y-Yes!”

“Hayato, you’re holding the lamb wrong. Like when we saw it during shearing, remember?”

“Oh, right.”

Following Haruki’s advice, Hayato adjusted his grip, setting the lamb’s rear on the ground and holding it from behind, as they’d seen during shearing. The lamb calmed down, and they sighed in relief.

“Heh, we did it, Haruki! Good thing Himeko’s okay too. Mom would’ve chewed me out for letting a girl get hurt!”

“Hmph, so it’s fine if I get hurt?”

Haruki pouted at Hayato’s teasing laughter.

“Huh? You’re different. I knew you’d be fine, partner!”

Haruki’s eyes widened at the word.

Partner.

Rolling the word in his mind, Haruki saw Hayato’s carefree grin and broke into a smile.

“Partner, huh? Alright then, heh!”

“Yeah! Thanks to you, we caught it!”

“Haha, yeah!”

“Hahaha!”

Their laughter soared into the blue sky.

It was the same as always. Always together.

Their clothes were caked in mud, their bare arms and legs scratched, their smiles brighter than the summer sun.

Ordinary, unremarkable days.

Countless memories built together.

This was one such memory, shining brightly.

A time when they knew nothing, simply existing side by side.

It was a story from a long-gone, hot summer day.
