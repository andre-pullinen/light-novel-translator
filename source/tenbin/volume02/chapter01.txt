Episode 1: The Definite Changes in This New Environment
Hayato woke with a sense of lethargy. The strong sunlight filtered through the curtains.

He vaguely recalled a dream but couldn’t remember it. Dreams were like that.

“Ugh, gotta get up…”

Shaking off the gloom, he scratched his head and got out of bed.

It was going to be another hot day.

The Kirishima siblings, ready early, walked to school. Himeko had woken up without fuss today.

“Alright, big bro, I’m off this way.”

“Yep.”

Exchanging weary voices under the heat, they parted at the crossroads.

Hayato sighed, looking up. The summer sun blazed, asserting its presence.

“So hot…”

City summers were tough.

Unlike rural Tsukinose, asphalt stretched endlessly. Instead of rustling leaves, air conditioners blew lukewarm air. Traffic lights didn’t just blink yellow.

Each step brought more sweat, his uniform sticking uncomfortably, worsening his mood.

But something else was weighing on him.

“Yo, Kirishima!”

“Morning, Mori.”

“Hey, transfer student!”

“Morning, Kirishima-kun.”

“There’s Kirishima. Sup.”

“…Morning.”

In the classroom, classmates greeted him with warm, teasing looks.

Last week, Haruki showed him a prank photo, and he’d grabbed her phone impulsively. Since then, everyone seemed to think he’d fallen hard for Nikaidou Haruki’s childhood friend.

Now, they were all curious about his “romance,” watching with amusement.

(It’s Himeko, my sister…)

Haruki, the culprit, had stammered, “P-people’s rumors last seventy-five days!” while avoiding his gaze.

“…Morning, Nikaidou.”

“G-Good morning, Kirishima-kun.”

His greeting to Haruki came out gruff, and he looked away. Haruki gave an apologetic smile.

Their awkwardness only fueled the class’s misunderstanding, though neither noticed.

“Hey, Nikaidou-san, got a sec?”

“We’ve got some questions!”

“Yes, what—!? Eek!”

Seeing Hayato’s flustered behavior, a group of girls swarmed Haruki, buzzing excitedly and sneaking glances at him.

They seemed like meddlesome types who loved stirring things up.

Hayato could see Haruki resisting, but the sparkling-eyed girls were relentless.

Soon, Haruki approached him, holding her phone with a guilty look.

“Um, Kirishima-kun…”

“…What?”

“So, about her… wanna see this?”

“Nah, I’m good.”

“Please, just look…?”

“…”

Hayato didn’t know what the girls had said. Haruki’s childhood friend was Himeko, his sister—seeing anything would just be awkward.

But her apologetic, troubled expression, and knowing she was playing the good girl, made it hard to brush her off.

He sighed heavily and glanced at the screen. Instead of Himeko’s photo, there was text:

“Sorry for everything. I’ll eat lunch with them today and clear up the misunderstanding!”

Looking at Haruki, she gave a wry smile and a wink, as if saying, “Leave it to me.”

She felt responsible, it seemed.

Haruki usually avoided conflict, eating lunch alone. Her willingness to fix this showed her resolve. Plus, last week at dinner, she’d apologized repeatedly for the prank.

(…Jeez.)

His expression softened, a small smile forming.

“Got it.”

“! Okay!”

Sensing his understanding, they shared a relieved look.

But then, “Kyaa!” came shrill squeals from behind.

“Eek!?”

The girls grabbed Haruki’s hands, dragging her off with an aura that brooked no resistance.

“Let’s talk, Nikaidou-san!”

“Just for a bit!”

“I was thinking, maybe I could help with something fun—er, I mean!”

Hayato watched, stunned, but noticed the girls were far friendlier with Haruki than when he’d first transferred.

Graceful as a peony, walking like a lily, Haruki was both popular and untouchable, a flower on a high peak. She’d kept walls up, which made sense.

But now, her vibe had shifted slightly. This scene proved it.

(That looks rough. Bet she’ll vent at dinner.)

Honestly, it made his chest feel weird, but at his place, Haruki still sprawled out carelessly, acting like old times.

A side she showed only to him. That thought made him chuckle.

“Kirishima, see something amazing about your sweetheart?”

“Mori? Nah, it’s not—”

Others interpreted his expression differently.

Mori, quick to notice, slung an arm around him, joined by grinning guys who weren’t letting him escape.

“Don’t we need more bonding, huh?”

“Yeah, Nikaidou-san’s childhood friend was cute, right?”

“So, what specifically got you hooked?”

“W-Wait, I—”

Ignoring his and Haruki’s confusion, the class buzzed with excitement.

Haruki had changed since his transfer.

And so had the world around Hayato.

Lunch break. Freed from dull classes, students reveled in liberty.

Voices rose across the classroom.

“Come on, Nikaidou-san, this way!”

“Bento? Cafeteria? We’re taking you either way!”

“I’ve always been curious about you, Nikaidou-san.”

“Uh, my bag—Eek!?”

A pack of starry-eyed girls whisked Haruki away like tigers with wings to their hangout.

Hayato mentally wished her luck and slipped off to their hideout.

Luckily, the hungry guys prioritized food over gossip, making his escape easy. Mori had bolted to the cafeteria the second the bell rang, ignoring the teacher.

Their hideout: a narrow, six-mat room in the old school building, now a storage space. Hayato and Haruki’s secret base and shelter.

Alone, Hayato looked around.

“…Pretty bare.”

Thanks to Haruki’s tidying, it was clean, but all it had was a broom by the door and two plain cushions.

Maybe that’s why it felt chilly, despite summer.

“Maybe I’ll grab some cushion covers…”

Voicing his thoughts, he opened his bento. Eating alone was mechanical, and he finished quickly. Checking the time, barely five minutes had passed, with plenty of lunch break left.

Normally, chatting with Haruki made time fly, but now it crawled, leaving him restless.

Looking around again, the room felt oddly vast.

(Haruki… was she always alone in here?)

Playing the good girl, living alone in a house. He had questions but didn’t want to spiral, so he scratched his head and stood.

He headed somewhere else.

Scorching lunch break. A flowerbed with neat rows at the school’s rear.

There, a petite girl with curly hair tended to vegetables.

“Yo, Mitake-san.”

“Oh, Kirishima-san!”

Despite the heat, Mitake Minamo worked diligently, but her face showed worry.

Glancing at the flowerbed, the vegetables looked wilted. Noticing his gaze, Minamo spoke hesitantly.

“I water them daily to keep the soil moist and prune as needed, but lately, they’ve been drooping…”

“You don’t need to water every day. Every few days, give a lot to soak deep. Daily small amounts evaporate, causing dehydration.”

“What…!?”

“But this isn’t dehydration. They’ve been blooming and fruiting a lot, right? That means they’ve used a lot of energy. So, what’s the issue?”

“E-Energy? Um…”

Minamo pondered his riddle, finger on her chin, tilting her head. Hayato squinted fondly.

(She’s so similar.)

Her curls and the vegetable blooms reminded him of Grandpa Gen’s sheep in Tsukinose, chasing flowers and fruits. It made him smile.

Then, Minamo exclaimed, “Ah!”

“They need nutrients—fertilizer!”

Her sparkling eyes screamed for praise, like a small animal. Hayato nearly patted her head but held back.

“Exactly. Vegetables get exhausted like people. Got fertilizer? I’ll help.”

“Like people… Oh, yes, we have some! Um…”

“Don’t put it directly on roots or stems. Sprinkle in a circle around them.”

“Yes! Here we go…”

The flowerbed was large but just a home garden. Together, they finished quickly.

Still, under the summer sun, Hayato was drenched, his uniform clinging.

“Done! Thank you!”

“A-Ah…!?”

“Something wrong…?”

Wiping sweat, he glanced at Minamo. Her blouse, like his, stuck to her, highlighting delicate but distinctly feminine curves. Despite her small frame, her chest was far more… prominent than Himeko’s and even Haruki’s. He quickly looked away.

Minamo tilted her head, puzzled by his reaction.

(…Caught off guard.)

She might remind him of sheep, but Minamo was a girl his age.

Unused to peers and avoiding such thoughts, her unguarded state flustered him.

Plus, despite her casual curls and lack of fashion, her face was adorably pretty.

A diamond in the rough.

“H-Hat!”

“Huh?”

“Like, a straw hat or something might be good. It’s hot, and heatstroke’s a risk.”

“Oh, you’re right. It’s been so hot lately.”

Taking his flustered words as advice, Minamo drooped, feeling careless. Hayato felt guilty for his excuse.

An awkward air settled until a hand towel appeared.

“Mitake-san, a hat’s good, but your clothes need work too. It’s a bit… distracting for boys.”

“Wha—Nikaidou?”



“Eh?”

Haruki, smiling, offered Minamo the towel.

Her gaze fixed on Minamo’s chest, where the wet blouse revealed lace patterns.

“P-Pyaaa!”

Realizing her state, Minamo’s face reddened. Clutching the towel to her chest, she fled.

Watching her go, Haruki turned to Hayato with a deadpan stare and muttered.

“…Pervert.”

“N-No, it’s not like—”

“…Lech.”

“W-Wait, I—”

“…”

“…Sorry.”

“Hmph!”

Her uncharacteristic words and attitude made Hayato apologize reflexively.

Afternoon classes.

“The Heijo-kyo capital was built in 710, modeled after Tang’s Chang’an, in present-day Nara—”

Japanese history class, with a lazy afternoon vibe.

But an uneasy aura came from Hayato’s neighbor.

“…Hmph.”

“…Sigh.”

Trying to check on her, Haruki turned away the moment he looked. He’d clearly upset her.

(…This is bad.)

The reason was obvious.

He’d stared at Minamo’s sweat-soaked uniform, specifically her chest.

It was instinctive for a teenage boy, but Har “‘Also, various systems were reformed, like the soyochō tax system… This is fun to compare with last month’s Asuka period. The handout—’”

“…Oh.”

He didn’t have that handout.

Almost a month since transferring, he’d settled in, but sometimes still needed Haruki’s help.

“Uh, Nikaidou… san?”

“…Hmph.”

Despite her sulk, Haruki sighed and slid her desk closer, showing him the handout.

(This happened when I first transferred, didn’t it?)

She’d been grumpy then too but still helped.

“Haha, thanks.”

“…Hmph.”

The memory made him chuckle.

But Haruki didn’t like that.

“Don’t look at this handout with the same lecherous eyes you used on that girl.”

“Wha!?”

Frowning and turning away, her soft but clear voice echoed in the classroom. After a brief silence, laughter erupted.

“Yo, what’d Kirishima do?”

“What kinda eyes were those?”

“Kirishima, no harassment! Back to class!”

“Grr…”

Amid the lingering laughter, Hayato shrank, blushing with embarrassment.

Haruki, still turned away, huffed.

After class, guys surrounded Hayato.

“Kirishima, what eyes did you give Nikaidou-san?”

“Was it about that childhood friend?”

“Did you see something that intense?”

“No, wait, I—”

Their curious eyes said they wouldn’t let him go without answers.

It was because of Haruki’s earlier attitude.

Pure, lovely, talented, and kind to all.

For her to scold someone like that was rare, sparking interest.

“Kirishima, what’s with the pervy eyes? Did you reveal some weird fetish to make Nikaidou say that?”

“Mori!?”

Mori tossed out a bombshell, egging on Hayato and the crowd.

“Got it… I’m into armpits!”

“I’m all about ankle lines.”

“…Collarbones.”

“No, I didn’t—!”

The guys’ talk about girls spiraled, heedless of the girls’ glares. The fetish debate, centered at Hayato’s desk, got more fuel from next door.

“Breasts. Kirishima-kun loves boobs.”

“Haru—Nikaidou!”

His voice was nearly a shout.

Guys cheered, “Ohh!” “Classic!” “Nikaidou saying ‘boobs’…!” while girls whispered, “Ugh, guys are the worst,” “Kirishima’s like that too…,” “But the photo girl… oh, that’s his type.”

The situation grew chaotic, like that morning.

Haruki pouted, lips pursed.

“Give me a break…”

Hayato slumped, head in hands.

The uproar turned into a pointless guy-girl argument, which he ignored. But Mori’s shocked voice reached him.

“…Nikaidou can make that face, huh.”

“Mori…?”

“Oh, Kirishima. Nah, nothing.”

Looking at Haruki, she was among the girls, giving the guys a fed-up look. Her eyes held a familiar mischievous glint.

(…What’s with her?)

It unsettled him.

Scratching his head to brush it off, he caught Haruki’s eye.

“—Hehe.”

“…Tch, that girl…”

She stuck out her tongue triumphantly, just for him.

Oddly, it felt… nice.

◇◇◇

City sunsets dyed buildings red, unlike Tsukinose’s mountains.

At a big convenience store on the road to the residential area, Himeko parted with classmates.

“Bye, Himeko-chan!”

“See ya, Kirishima-san!”

“Later!”

“Yeah, see you all tomorrow.”

Early-lit streetlights brightened the city dusk, but Himeko’s steps were heavy.

(Ate too much again… Ugh, tasty but pricey…)

Rubbing her stomach, she moved slowly.

The cause: convenience store sweets. In Tsukinose, snacks were just long-shelf-life candies at a hobbyist shop.

Here, stores brimmed with creamy, short-shelf-life desserts.

Himeko, weak to sweets, couldn’t resist.

Her classmates egged her on, making her pocket money situation dire.

“Haa, I’m home…?”

“Yo, welcome back.”

“…Welcome back, Hime-chan.”

Himeko tilted her head upon entering.

Hayato chopped vegetables with a troubled look, while Haruki sat on the sofa, clutching a cushion to her chest, sulking. A tense air hung between them.

Haruki, barefoot, sat cross-legged, her guard so lax her underwear was visible from the right angle. Himeko sighed, layered with meaning.

“Uh… Haru-chan, what’s going on?”

“Hime-chan… Hayato’s a beast…”

“Huh?”

Haruki’s serious tone and tight grip on the cushion made Himeko’s cool-headedness vanish.

“Did he… touch you!? I knew he was a beast, but Haru-chan, you’re pretty stacked, right!? Can I touch a bit—please share the blessings!”

“H-Hime-chan!? No, not me, it was the garden—Eek, my stomach!”

“Hey, stop it, Himeko.”

Somehow misinterpreting, Himeko lunged to check Haruki’s chest.

Hayato pulled her off, his eyes half-serious.

“Half… no, a third is fine, please share!”

“No way!”

“Come on!”

Hayato sighed deeply, frowning.

“Haha, got it. Big bro was eyeing a girl’s chest, huh.”

“He was so obvious about it.”

“…Give me a break.”

Dinner talk was Himeko and Haruki teasing Hayato.

Himeko’s misunderstanding was cleared, and Haruki’s tone had shifted to playful jabs. Hayato, getting it, gave a wry smile.

The main dish: diced eggplant stir-fried with ground meat, garlic, maitake mushrooms, and green chilies, seasoned sweet-spicy with soy sauce, mirin, oyster sauce, and a touch of miso—a twist on mapo eggplant.

Perfect with rice, Hayato piled it on his bowl. Haruki and Himeko followed, eating heartily.

“By the way, Haru-chan, is that girl’s chest that big?”

“Hmm, she’s petite, but definitely two… no, three ranks above me?”

“What!? You’re already two ranks above me!”

“Haha, honestly, my eyes went there too.”

“Hmph, can’t blame him then. I forgive you, big bro.”

“…Yeah, yeah, eat your food, not just talk.”

““Okay!””

After eating and sipping tea, things were back to normal as Haruki prepared to leave. While Hayato washed dishes, she sprawled on the sofa, watching TV as boldly as Himeko.

(Oh, I get it.)

Himeko realized Haruki’s mood.

She’d been upset about the daytime incident and wanted a way to act normal again. Himeko had been played.

“Haru-chan, you’ve always been bad at being honest.”

“W-What’s that, Hime-chan?”

“Nothing~”

Himeko glanced at Hayato, exasperated. Haruki, feeling seen through, started getting ready.

“Uh, yeah, gotta go. Thanks for dinner!”

“Sure, take care.”

“Haru-chan…”

Haruki got up, slipping on her discarded socks.

Recalling their talk, Himeko muttered casually.

“Big bro’s hopeless, huh. Two cute girls here, and he’s eyeing others.”

“Haha, right? He should settle for me and be happy… with…”

“Though, if he looked at us like that, it’d be weird—Haru-chan?”

“…”

Haruki froze, one sock on, the other half-pulled, looking ridiculous.

Even Himeko grew suspicious after ten seconds of stillness.

“Haru-chan? Hello, Haru-chan?”

“—! H-Hime-chan! Haha… uh, I’m heading out! No need to see me off, bye!”

“Haru-chan!?”

Snapped back by Himeko’s voice, Haruki bolted out, socks uneven, in a flustered rush.

Himeko, stunned, and Hayato, pausing dishwashing, gaped.

“She left?”

“Yeah, seems so.”

“Shouldn’t you walk her home?”

“She probably thought you’d stare at her chest.”

“…As if. Stop joking and take a bath, it’s ready.”

“Got it.”

Trading quips, Himeko headed to the bathroom, grabbing a towel and clothes. Her earlier jest lingered—maybe it wasn’t wrong.

(Is big bro looking at Haru-chan like… Nah, no way.)

She dismissed it.

Haruki’s ultra-relaxed state at their place was like Himeko’s. Hayato never seemed to see her romantically, more like family.

(Or… is Haru-chan starting to notice him? Haha, no way.)

That thought made her chest twinge, so she pushed it away, stripping off her sailor uniform.

Spotting the scale, unused for a while, her slightly rounded belly caught her eye. After a moment’s hesitation, she stepped on.

“~~~~~~~!!?!?!?”

A silent scream.

“Himeko, what’s wrong!?”

“Idiot, don’t come, don’t peek, stupid bro!”

“S-Sorry!”

Crouching in shame, Himeko clutched her head in panic.

(W-W-What do I do…!?)

The scale, checked after ten days, showed a near 10% weight gain, thanks to her recent sweets binge.

Late night, the waning moon yet to rise.

Streetlights kept the path bright despite no moonlight.

“Hah, hah, hah…!”

A girl ran, hair wild, sprinting full speed.

An odd sight.

Her face, red to her neck, would’ve drawn eyes in daylight.

“I’m home!”

Haruki shouted her usual greeting to the dark, but unusually, didn’t wait for a reply, diving into the bathroom.

Tossing her sweaty blouse into the washer, she doused herself with cold shower water.

“Cold!?”

Muttering the obvious, she continued as the water warmed. Sweat washed away, but the haze in her chest lingered.

Lately, small things kept unsettling her.

“Ugh…”

Under hot water, she recalled the day.

After dodging the girls’ interrogation and heading to the hideout, it was empty. Knowing Hayato’s habits, she went to the vegetable garden, and there he was.

“Hay… Kirish—”

She tried to call out but couldn’t.

Hayato tended the garden expertly, occasionally guiding Minamo, the gardening club girl, in a gentle tone. She asked questions eagerly, relying on him.

A side of Hayato Haruki didn’t know.

(Yeah… Hayato’s always been a caretaker…)

Memories flashed: promising to eat lunch together at the hideout, him escorting her home, insisting she stay over to avoid her house.

What he was doing was his virtue, something she admired. Yet, her chest stung.

Confused, she studied Minamo.

She knew little about her—different middle schools. Quiet, much shorter, but with notable curves. A cute, almost animal-like charm.

Something girlish Haruki lacked around Hayato.

(What…)

An unpleasant feeling hit, undefined. Watching Hayato smile with Minamo, irritation grew.

When he blushed at her larger chest, Haruki’s patience snapped, and she barged in. Her later attitude, she admitted, was childish, just sulking.

Himeko’s reaction surprised but grateful her. She thought her feelings would reset by tomorrow.

“Haha, right? He should settle for me and be happy… with…”

A casual chat revealed the root of her unease.

(I thought Hayato might be taken!)

A childish, possessive urge, not quite jealousy.

She knew Hayato was a normal teenage boy and teased him for it. She trusted he understood her.

Still, she hated him blushing at another girl.

(I’m not exactly small…)

Trying to shake the thought, she turned up the shower, but it was already maxed.

“Ugh, enough!”

Scrubbing hard to dispel the haze, her heart wouldn’t calm.

It’d be a long shower.

“So hot~”

Overlong showering left her overheated.

Unable to stand it, she grabbed ice cream, returned to her room, and cranked the AC.

“Ow, but yummy!”

Biting too fast gave a brief headache, but she devoured it.

Lingering turmoil made it stress-eating.

(It’s all Hayato’s fault!)

After emotional turmoil, she blamed him. Unfair and immature, but she couldn’t help it.

Her heart was a mess.

Hayato always threw her off, same as ever. But things weren’t the same. Still, it wasn’t bad—she was most exasperated with herself.

“…Haa.”

Sighing with the ice cream stick in her mouth, she sat cross-legged, looking around.

Manga, games, a half-built model, new cosmetics, fashion magazines, and girlish clothes lay scattered, lonely.

“…”

The room was silent. The usual, familiar quiet for years.

Yet, she compared it to the noisy Kirishima apartment, frowning, her chest restless.

Ring~

“!”

Her rarely ringing phone startled her. She grabbed it.

“…Haru-chan.”

“Uh, Hime-chan? W-What’s up?”

“…You okay?”

“Uh, what do you mean…?”

Himeko’s voice was dark, serious. Haruki didn’t get it.

It felt like her heart was laid bare, her pulse racing.

“…I gained [X] kilos.”

“—!”

She went speechless. She had suspicions.

“Haru-chan, you had seconds today too, right…?”

“Ah, ah, ah…!”

Lately, eating at Hayato’s felt so good.

Unlike her mechanical meals, eating together, talking, made her eat more, take seconds.

And at home, she’d stress-eat ice cream, like now.

“How about you?”

“Wait, hold on, I’m probably fine? Yeah, definitely.”

“Face reality. Let’s fix it before it’s too late. Weigh yourself now.”

“Haha, I’m fine…”

But she’d noticed her waist and arms feeling off.

Talking, she returned to the bathroom, set her phone down, and hesitated before the scale. Stripping completely—a futile resistance for a few grams.

“Kyaaa!!?!?!?”

Reality was harsh.

Like Himeko, Haruki’s weight was up nearly 10% since last checked.

“Hehe, diet alliance formed, huh?”

Himeko’s voice, darkly eager to drag her in, echoed from the phone.

Haruki’s body, like her heart, was changing, her budding feelings spiraling further.
