I can compromise on other things. But when a theme park date is on the line, I refuse to compromise. I want to come out on top.

“And besides, I already lost to Eito the other day…”

I’d steeled myself to ask for a reward, but in the end, I was the one who was soundly defeated by Eito. Considering more thieving cats will keep appearing from now on, I really want to go on the offensive here.

“…”

“Understood.”

“…………….”

Just the other day, I thought I was on the attack, only to be forced into a disadvantage by Eito’s “teasing.”

“…Well. True, I suppose? It’s possible I’ve been spinning my wheels a little… until now.”

“A little…?”

“Hey, now. Don’t add a question mark.”

I bet she’s tilting that pretty head of hers charmingly.

It was easy to imagine just how beautiful and cute her expression must be.

It’s partly bravado, but it’s not just bravado.

In reality, I’ve approached Eito in various ways so far. But all of them failed… no. I was soundly defeated each time. That’s why I’m changing my approach. Changing my methods.

If I’ve failed by going first, then I’ll dare to go second.

I’ll simply seize the initiative after she makes her move.

“This is a challenge. A fair and square one.”

“I accept.”
